Today's Poetry

Day 1.

Friendship, to me, has never been about convenience or circumstance, it is the quiet disruption, the subtle shift in your soul, where a person becomes more than a presence, they become a rhythm that matches your own. When the world weighed me down with silence, you were the unexpected laugh that cracked open the walls I had built, you were the beacon I did not know I was steering toward. They say friendship is lighter than love, but if love is fire, then friendship is the hearth, steady, warm, and enduring in ways passion cannot always be. You became the oasis in my desert of self-doubt, the lighthouse when the storms of solitude raged. Our conversations carved paths in my mind, etched new maps in my memory, our laughter planted gardens in desolate corners, and our silences, they spoke like scriptures, unbroken, unwavering, true. So when I define friendship, I define you. Not as a fleeting bond, but as the one who walked in unannounced, and instead of turning my schemes on their head, you built them with me, brick by brick, until what once was fragile became unshakable. And if there is fate in love, then in friendship there is choice, and you chose me, as I chose you, and for that, I call you not friend alone, but keeper of my storms, my joy. My only constant in a world of change.
