Today's Poetry

Day 5.

Passion does not knock softly, it arrives like wildfire, igniting everything in its path. You were not a whisper, you were a blaze, and I, willingly, was the forest. You consumed me, yet in the devouring, I was reborn. If love is the steady sun, then passion is the comet, rare, dangerous, but unforgettable. We are storm and silence, a universe collapsing into itself, only to explode again with greater force. Our passion may not be eternal, but it is immortal in my memory, etched into the marrow of who I am. For though fires dim, the ashes remain warm, and in that warmth, I am reminded that once, I was wholly alive, wholly yours, burning in the only way one can, when love becomes flame.
