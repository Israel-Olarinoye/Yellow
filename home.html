Our Love Story

Poems to my Yellow

Friendship

Friendship, to me, has never been about convenience or circumstance, it is the quiet disruption, the subtle shift in your soul...

Kinship

Blood is an easy word, a simple tether that ties names on paper, but kinship to me, is not merely who you are born to, it is who my soul recognizes as home...

Love

Love was not a storm that tore through me, it was a dawn that crept gently, spilling gold across the solitude I swore I would protect...

Adoration

My definition of love is someone being able to make all your plans stop because they simply are there or exist...

Passion

Passion does not knock softly, it arrives like wildfire, igniting everything in its path...

Grief

Could I have been more? Done better, I ponder as I filter through the archives, ones filled with love, laughter, and a bond once perceived unbreakable...

Reconciliation

We meet again, though differently than before. Where once we stood on the same side of the shore, we now face each other across broken bridges...

Life

If I were to name life, I would call it a tapestry, woven with threads I never see until I traced them backward...
