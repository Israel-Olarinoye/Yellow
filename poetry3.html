Today's Poetry

Day 3.

Love was not a storm that tore through me, it was a dawn that crept gently, spilling gold across the solitude I swore I would protect. I had made plans to shut out the world, to remain untouched, unbothered. But then you came, and suddenly all those plans lay scattered at your feet, and I did not mourn them, because they were never worth more than the sound of your voice. You were not unexpected, for even if I had not known you before, my soul must have, because the moment you appeared, everything felt premeditated, as though fate had whispered my name to yours long before we walked this earth. Your smile became my sunrise, your eyes my night sky, a galaxy that swallowed me whole. Your presence rekindled what I thought was long dead: a tenderness, a fire, a yearning to belong not just to myself but to someone who called me “theirs.” And so when I call you my good thing, "nkan rere mi", it is not just scripture, it is testimony. You are love not because I needed it, but because I was destined to find it in you. And now that I have, every breath is a hymn, every glance a prayer, every heartbeat the vow: I love you, I love you, I love you, in ways words will never cage.
