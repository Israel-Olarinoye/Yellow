Today's Poetry

Day 6.

"Could I have been more? Done better", I ponder as I filter through the archives, ones filled with love, laughter, and a bond once perceived unbreakable. Love is funny, a concept so deep it makes even my reasoning fail, and for all my wits, my words cannot even encompass what it means, what it brings. People always told me about what happens when people fall in love, the happiness, laughter, joy, fulfillment, confidence, and growth, that being loved right brings to a person. What they did not warn me about is what happens when that love is gone. ‎ ‎I suddenly find myself thrown into a room that mirrors what was once felt, but the disparity is so harsh, it's almost cruel. I suddenly have echoes of memories, fond and dear, but they bring such a wave of grief they're so painful to remember. ‎ ‎If my love were a scarf knitted with the love of a grandmother, now that scarf resembles one that has been ripped almost to shreds by a feral toddler, on it's last threads, not serving any real purpose, only there to remind me of what once existed, something I can no longer grasp. ‎ ‎If it were a town, full of lights, the warmth only an intimate community can bring, the familiarity only those who have known each other for lifetimes can have, it's now a barren land, filled with charred remains of walls constructed with words of love and encouragement, streets that were once full of joy now despondent, a dark town filled with familiar spirits, not here to propagate familiarity as their name would suggest, but to shroud me with shadows of the past, remind me of what was, and painfully, what could have been. ‎ ‎If it were a garden, full of color, vibrant with a cacophony of noises only Gaia's love can bring, it's now a desolate plain, full of weeds of neglect and starvation, starved of the love that once gave this garden color. ‎ ‎It's kind of ironic, insane even, that one person could bring so much light and when they're gone, it's gone with them, that light that once powered my heart just fades into obscurity, and all that's left is hope, a flicker, a spark, hope that the love that kept this little room in my heart alight, this boat in my soul afloat, will come back one day and devour me wholly as it once did. This flicker remains, however little, not because you will come back, but because that love was so pure a piece of it always remains with me, that piece is that little light that flickers, and I hope it doesn't sputter and die one day, because I want to keep that spark, my little reminder of what it was to be loved and wanted, fully and genuinely by you, my love. ‎ ‎
