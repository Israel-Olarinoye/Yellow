Today's Poetry

Day 7.

We meet again, though differently than before. Where once we stood on the same side of the shore, we now face each other across broken bridges, splinters beneath our feet, silence between our words. But reconciliation is not the erasure of wounds, it is the art of weaving scars into strength. The threads of fate frayed when we pulled too hard, but perhaps they can be wound anew, not to erase what was, but to honor it. I do not seek perfection, nor the naïve dawn we once believed eternal. What I seek is the courage to look at ruins and see foundations, to meet your eyes and say: we can still build, if we choose. Perhaps the garden will never bloom as it once did, but even barren soil can be tilled again, even burned towns can be lit with new fires, even broken bridges can be walked upon carefully, if both of us are willing. And maybe this time, reconciliation will not be fate’s gift, but our, the quiet decision to begin again.
