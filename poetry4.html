Today's Poetry

Day 4.

‎My definition of love is someone being able to make all your plans stop because they simply are there or exist. ‎I define love this way because that's what you did. ‎It's kind of funny how the original plan was to focus on myself solely and leave all external affairs aside till you walked in and turned my scheme on its head. ‎I mean people say love finds you when you least expect but this wasn't unexpected, it was premeditated albeit unconscious. ‎During the periods where I was meant to be in my solitude, I found myself gravitating towards the only beacon of light, the one who was an oasis in a desert, a lighthouse on a stormy sea. ‎That alone couldn't have been enough for sure, because I was determined to stay alone for a long time, but as time passed and our conversations deepened and my knowledge of you deepened, I was unknowingly rekindling what I originally thought was dead in me. ‎There's this theory that people are only meant to meet as lovers at the right time, they're apparently meant to be connected by the thread of fate, while that may hold, I'd like to believe that I wound the threads of fate till they spelled out your name. ‎Now that I've found you, my good thing, my person, I thank the universe everyday because it also conspired for me to find you. ‎You, the girl with a smile that looks like Hyperion's chariot as it makes its route across the sky each day. ‎You, the girl with eyes that look like black holes, holding the mysterious and unexplored beauties of this world. ‎You, the girl with a soul that belongs in Elysium, not in this depraved place we call home, earth. ‎You, the girl who had a lot of choices but still chose me. ‎I call you my good thing because the Holy Scriptures say "he who finds a wife finds a good thing" and I've found you my good thing. ‎I was meant to find you, to love you, every piece of me having a piece of you, even when I have no other words; I love you, in a way that can exist without needing to be expressed, but I can't help but want you to hear it, I love you deeply. ‎‎My definition of love is someone being able to make all your plans stop because they simply are there or exist. ‎I define love this way
